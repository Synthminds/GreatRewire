# B5 OVERLAY RATIONALE: 20 printed against a model draft of 12

**Date:** 2026-08-01
**Forecast:** B5, "China and Hong Kong together account for 12% or more of NVIDIA's total
revenue in fiscal 2028"
**Monte Carlo draft:** 12
**Printed:** 20
**Guardrail:** G3, which requires a dated written rationale alongside any printed probability set
against the model's own posterior. This document is that rationale.

---

## Why the model draft is 12

`analysis/50_mc_finals.py` prices B5 off the network mechanism the rest of the slate is built on:
severed maximum-weight edges do not re-heal. The China-US corridor is the maximum-weight cross-bloc
edge in the 2024 network, the empirical null puts it at approximately 0.86 times its benchmark
median and roughly the 34th percentile of comparable pairs, and the deletion-law check in
`analysis/70_rf_rigor.py` finds substantial edges dying at roughly 2.84% against the 0.50% the
fitted law generates. An edge severed that hard, in a model fitted on structural persistence,
recovers slowly. Twelve is the right number for that question.

## Why the printed number is higher

The model answers a structural question. B5 resolves on an administrative one, and four
considerations separate them.

**The trees carry no term for administrative reversal.** Every feature in the forecast's parameter
distribution describes network structure or measured trade. None of them encodes that this
particular edge is throttled by two revocable policy instruments rather than by distance, cost, or
capacity. The overlay is the price of that missing term, and it is a judgment rather than a
measurement.

**This is a near-threshold bet, not a tail bet.** The FY2026 10-K puts China and Hong Kong at 9.1%
of revenue with data-center compute at zero. The threshold is 12%. Clearing it requires
approximately 2.9 percentage points over two fiscal years on a base that already exists. A model
that treats 12% as a distant state is pricing the wrong distance.

**The edge is double-throttled, and two throttles mean two independent release paths.** Washington
licenses the chips; Beijing caps the buying, reported below 200,000 units and training-only. Either
throttle releasing on its own moves the number, and they are controlled by different governments
answering to different pressures. A structural model sees one blocked edge. What is actually there
is a bilateral administrative arrangement whose reversal time is short against an FY2028 horizon.

**The basis is billing location, and it cuts the other way.** B5 resolves on the FY2028
geographic-segment note, which reports revenue by customer billing location rather than by end
use. Chips billed to a Singapore or Hong Kong intermediary and deployed elsewhere count where they
are billed, so the reported China and Hong Kong line can move without any change in Chinese
end-use, and can equally stay flat while end-use rises. This is a genuine argument against the
overlay: it means the threshold can be cleared or missed for reasons that have nothing to do with
whether the severed edge is re-healing, which is the thing B5 exists to test. It is also why the
overlay stops at 20 rather than running higher.

**The pre-control base rate sits above the threshold.** Before the controls, China and Hong Kong
ran well above 12% of revenue. Clearing 12% by FY2028 is therefore a partial recovery toward a
level this corridor has already sustained, not a move to a level it has never held. The model's 12
implicitly prices it as the latter.

## Why not higher than 20

The bounding argument runs the other way and is the reason the overlay stops where it does.

The paper's own finding is that severed maximum-weight edges do not re-heal, and B5 exists to test
that finding rather than to hedge it. Both ends now want the throttle for their own reasons:
Washington for export control, Beijing for domestic-substitution policy. A number much above 20
would price a bilateral policy reversal that neither side has signalled, and it would contradict
the framework the rest of the slate rests on. Twenty prices roughly a one-in-five chance that at
least one of the two throttles releases far enough, far enough ahead of the FY2028 close, to move
a reported revenue share by three points. It does not price a return to the pre-control regime.

## What would retire this rationale

If either government publicly relaxes its instrument before the FY2028 filing, the administrative
term this overlay adds is no longer a judgment and becomes measurable. At that point B5 should be
re-priced on the evidence rather than on this note. The reverse also holds: a codified,
non-discretionary ban at either end collapses the overlay, and B5 should return toward the model
draft.

## Status

This is the largest relative overlay on the slate at approximately 67% above the model draft, and
the paper says so in Appendix D rather than leaving a reader to compute it. It is the slate's
weakest seam and is labelled as one. Endogenizing policy-reversal tempo, so that the model carries
the term this note supplies by hand, is the next piece of work on the forecasting side.
